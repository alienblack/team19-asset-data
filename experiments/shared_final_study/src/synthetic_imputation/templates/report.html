01 / Evaluation design

Data, conditions and scoring

Controlled comparison

Fit on four measured row folds, predict the fifth, then rotate through all five. Models share folds and hidden cells. The clean matching truth is used for scoring rather than fitting.

The original nine conditions cross 10%, 20% and 30% missingness with 0%, 5% and 10% noise. Additional experiments test grouped gaps, age/value-dependent gaps, recording errors, heavier missingness, spatial holdout and predictor ablations.

The reference condition shown below is {{ reference }}. The separate Subteam A benchmark uses a different split, masking rate and confidence protocol.

How to read the measures

Numeric skill = 1 − MAE(method) / MAE(median). Zero matches the median, one is perfect, and negative is worse. The mean gives each of {{ field_count }} numeric fields equal weight.

Model-ID accuracy measures correct equipment categories. It is kept separate from numeric skill.

Point-fill limitation. A low average error does not guarantee field tolerance, preserve a distribution or validate a per-record confidence interval.

06 / Interpretation and run details

Scope, limitations and reproduction

Interpretation limits. One synthetic population and designed gaps do not establish performance on the live SAPN register. This shared study evaluates point predictions. It does not validate per-asset uncertainty intervals. Paired error-comparison intervals are a different quantity.
