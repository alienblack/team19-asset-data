How to choose field settings

For an error of up to 5 years, choose Absolute and enter 5. For an error of up to 10%, choose Relative and enter 10. Leave tolerance unset when it has not been agreed.

Advanced settings let you set limits, round integer fills and name field groups. For grouped gaps, give related fields the same group name. Control excludes a numeric test field from mean skill while retaining its individual results.
